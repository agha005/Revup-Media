Aussies Merch

IndustryMerchandise
ScopeEmail Creative
FormatPromotion
ResultsPending Evidence

The Opportunity

The supplied Aussies Merch campaign combines supporter imagery, a direct discount, social proof and a multi-product collection. The opportunity was to keep a long promotional email energetic while still making the offer and product choices easy to scan.

Visible campaign goals
  • Lead with a recognizable supporter moment.
  • Make the GAMEON10 offer prominent and repeatable.
  • Move from proof into a clear featured-product section.

System We Built

The design uses a strong blue, red, white and black visual system taken from the brand artwork. The content moves through a clear sequence: attention, discount, supporting proof, featured collection and final store information.

What the creative includes
  • High-impact hero imagery.
  • Repeated promotional messaging.
  • Two customer-review blocks.
  • A structured featured-product grid.

The Outcome

The completed creative provides a cohesive long-form promotional email with a clear offer hierarchy and multiple routes into the product collection.

Results remain evidence based
