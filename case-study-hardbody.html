Case study 02

HardBody.

A high-contrast product campaign that moves from a direct performance problem to product education, social proof and a clearly organized collection.

01 — Background

What the approved artwork shows.

The supplied HardBody design presents lifting accessories through a bold black, white and red visual system. The campaign opens with a direct performance message, explains the friction the product addresses, adds a customer testimonial and closes with a product collection.

The page does not claim the original commercial brief or campaign outcome because those details were not supplied. Its analysis stays limited to the visible creative.

Transparent case-study policy

Campaign data can be added once screenshots or source records are available. No revenue, conversion or engagement number has been invented.

02 — Challenge

Turn product features into a stronger reason to act.

The visible campaign must explain why the product matters before presenting the range. It uses a problem-solution sequence so the reader understands the performance context, sees supporting proof and then reaches the product options.

01

Own the contrast

The black, white and red palette creates a consistent and recognizable visual system.

02

Name the problem

Short problem statements connect the product to familiar training frustrations.

03

Support the claim

A customer testimonial adds context before the full collection appears.

04

Make choice simple

Four color variants are organized into a clear grid with repeated product actions.
