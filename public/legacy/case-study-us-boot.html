Case study

US Boot

IndustryFootwear
ScopeFlows + Campaigns
Build Time5 Days
ResultsGenerated Revenue 15.06%

The Opportunity

US Boot was preparing for a Fox News feature without email automations or pop-up forms in place to capture and convert incoming traffic.

System We Built

Within five days, five Klaviyo flows, campaign email support and an optimized pop-up were launched to turn the incoming visitors into a long-term retention channel.

The Results

After the flows and campaigns were set up, email generated 15.06% of total revenue for the displayed period.
