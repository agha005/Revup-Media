The Opportunity

Twinky needed campaign emails that felt warm, practical and recognisably theirs - while giving parents a clear reason to explore the products at each stage of family life.

System We Built

The full case-study layout is already prepared for the verified story: project background, customer problem, campaign approach, visual decisions and implementation notes.

The Results

We created both email flows and campaigns for Twinky. The supplied dashboard shows email generated 29.47% of total revenue, with flows contributing the majority of attributed email revenue.

ESP performance evidence

For the displayed period, €14,842.61 was attributed to email. Flows accounted for 90.11% of attributed email revenue, while campaigns contributed 9.89%.
