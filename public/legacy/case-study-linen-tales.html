Case study

Linen Tales

BrandLinen Tales
ScopeFlows + Campaigns
FormatComplete Email Setup
ResultsGenerated Revenue 36.9%

The Opportunity

The supplied Linen Tales emails needed to keep a calm, premium visual tone while guiding customers toward seasonal arrivals, curated categories and timely offers.

Visible campaign goals
  • Use thoughtful lifestyle imagery to lead each message.
  • Keep product paths clear without overwhelming the reader.
  • Adapt promotional messages to lifecycle moments.

System We Built

The collection uses a soft editorial layout, warm product photography and restrained calls to action. Each email balances a clear commercial message with the brand's considered home-and-linen aesthetic.

What the creative includes
  • New-arrival product storytelling.
  • Seasonal dining collection content.
  • Subscriber-only promotion messaging.
  • First-time post-purchase recommendations.

The Results

After setting up the complete email program - flows, campaigns and the supporting email structure - Linen Tales generated 36.9% of total revenue through email.

ESP performance evidence

The supplied Omnisend dashboard shows €43,766.58 from email, representing 36.9% of store revenue for the displayed period.
