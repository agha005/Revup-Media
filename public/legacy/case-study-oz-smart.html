The Brand

OZ Smart brings together hats, umbrellas and outdoor accessories for everyday life outdoors. The email creative uses product photography, practical details and a warm orange accent to keep the brand recognisable throughout the customer journey.

The Email System

The design collection shows welcome, browse, cart, checkout and thank-you emails. Together, these designs introduce the range, bring shoppers back to products they viewed and support customers after purchase.
