Case study

OZ Smart

IndustryOutdoor accessories
ScopeLifecycle email design
CreativeWelcome and shopping journeys
PortfolioOZ Smart flows

The Opportunity

OZ Smart needed a more connected email journey for people shopping hats, umbrellas and outdoor accessories. The priority was to introduce the brand clearly, bring shoppers back to products they considered and make each message feel recognisably OZ Smart.

System We Built

The lifecycle email designs connect the moments from first signup through shopping and purchase. Product photography, practical details and the brand’s orange accent give the collection a consistent visual language.

Key areas of work
  • Welcome flow email design.
  • Browse and site abandonment emails.
  • Cart and checkout recovery emails.
  • Thank-you and post-purchase emails.
  • Consistent product and offer presentation across the journey.

The Results
